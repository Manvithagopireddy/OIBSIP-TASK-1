Key Features

What Sets It
Apart

🖥️

Micro-Edge FHD Display

The 14-inch Full HD IPS micro-edge display delivers crisp, vivid visuals with slim bezels that maximise your screen real estate. Whether you're editing photos, watching movies, or video-calling, every detail shines with 250-nit brightness and anti-glare coating.

  • 14" FHD IPS
  • 250 nits
  • Anti-glare
  • Micro-Edge
  • sRGB Tuned
🧠

Intel Core i5 / i7

11th or 12th Gen Intel Core processors deliver fast, responsive performance for multitasking, content creation, and everyday productivity.

🎮

Intel Iris Xe Graphics

Integrated Intel Iris Xe delivers surprisingly capable graphics for video editing, light gaming, and smooth 4K streaming. Optional NVIDIA MX550 available.

💾

8GB / 16GB DDR4

Up to 16GB of 3200 MHz DDR4 RAM keeps dozens of browser tabs, apps, and creative tools running smoothly — no slowdowns.

🔋

All-Day Battery

Up to 10 hours of real-world battery life. HP Fast Charge gets you from 0 to 50% in just 45 minutes — perfect for busy days.

⌨️

Comfort & Security

A backlit keyboard ensures comfortable typing in any environment. The integrated fingerprint reader provides instant, secure login — no passwords needed. The large, smooth touchpad makes navigation effortless.

  • Backlit Keyboard
  • Fingerprint Reader
  • HP Privacy Camera
🔌

Full Connectivity

USB-C (with charging), USB-A 3.2, HDMI 2.0, Wi-Fi 6, and Bluetooth 5.0 — connected to everything, everywhere.

🪶

Premium Aluminium Build

Natural Silver aluminium chassis — lightweight at just 1.41 kg with a refined finish that looks as good as it performs.

🪟

Ready Out of the Box

Windows 11 Home comes pre-installed alongside HP Support Assistant, HP Audio Switch, and McAfee LiveSafe — everything you need to hit the ground running from day one.

  • Windows 11 Home
  • HP Support Assistant
  • McAfee LiveSafe
