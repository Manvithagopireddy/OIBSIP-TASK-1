About the Machine

Crafted for
Modern Life

The HP Pavilion 14 is engineered for the way you live and work today. Its slim aluminium chassis and micro-edge display pack a stunning visual experience into a form factor you can take anywhere — from the office to the café to the classroom.

Powered by Intel Core i5/i7 processors with optional Intel Iris Xe or NVIDIA MX graphics, the Pavilion 14 handles creative tasks, multitasking, and everyday computing with quiet confidence. All-day battery means you stay productive without the anxiety of finding a socket.
